fork.

Contributions of code, documentation, artwork and bug reports are welcome. Open an
issue before starting a large change so the approach can be agreed first. For work on
the pronunciation list, read [AGENTS.md](AGENTS.md) before editing anything under
`src/tts/lexicon/`.
